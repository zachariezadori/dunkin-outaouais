Context and next steps

What Foodtastic is looking for

  • Units of 500 to 1,500 sq ft with a drive-thru.
  • M Immobilier is their exclusive real estate partner in Quebec, with over 100 sites under review.
  • About 1,400 franchisee applications in 10 days: showing up with a site already scouted helps win the territory.
  • The Outaouais isn't named anywhere in their public announcements.

The retail space market

  • Downtown Hull: 19.3% of storefronts empty, the worst commercial street in Quebec for the second year in a row.
  • Île de Hull: 56 of 257 storefronts empty. A third are empty on promenade du Portage and rue Hôtel-de-Ville.
  • Vacancy rates: Saint-Joseph 4.9%, Gréber 2.7%, chemin d'Aylmer–rue Principale 1.9% (little choice in Aylmer).
  • City of Gatineau: up to $50,000 reimbursed for interior work, equipment and signage in some areas (Eddy, Notre-Dame, Buckingham; Principale and Gréber planned). Need to check whether a franchise qualifies.

Next steps

  1. Visit the top 5 on a weekday morning, 7 to 9 a.m. Count cars and see which side of the road the commuters to Ottawa use.
  2. Call the brokers for 774 ch. d'Aylmer and 74–78 Gréber, then First Capital about Carrefour du Plateau.
  3. Find the owner of 1056 Maloney Ouest in the city's property assessment roll.
  4. Check with the City's planning department whether a drive-thru is allowed.
  5. Send 3 sites to Foodtastic with the territory request.
