Context and next steps

What we searched

  • Listings on Centris, LogisQuébec, DuProprio, Kijiji, plus brokers and developers.
  • 2025–2026 closures in Le Droit, TVA Gatineau, Radio-Canada, 104.7, the Aylmer Bulletin and the Gatineau Bulletin.
  • City documents: approved demolitions, projects with a drive-thru (Destination Vanier).
  • The 23 Tim Hortons in the official Gatineau directory, with each site's distance to the nearest one.

What Foodtastic is looking for

  • Units of 500 to 1,500 sq ft with a drive-thru.
  • M Immobilier is their exclusive real estate partner in Quebec.
  • About 1,400 franchisee applications in 10 days: showing up with a site already scouted helps.
  • The Outaouais isn't named anywhere in their public announcements.

Next steps

  • Visit the top 5 on a weekday morning, 7 to 9 a.m.
  • Call Via Capitale (774 ch. d'Aylmer) and First Capital (Carrefour du Plateau).
  • Find the owners of 261 Maloney Est and 1056 Maloney Ouest in the assessment roll.
  • Check with the City's planning department whether a drive-thru is allowed.
  • Send 3 sites to Foodtastic with the territory request.
