Context and next steps

How we picked the corners

  • The City of Gatineau's 7,383 traffic counts (2016 to 2023) and the MTQ's 2025 counts on Allumettières and Highway 50: every intersection ranked by volume.
  • We kept the corners on the morning route to Ottawa and the highways, then looked at what sits on each corner: gas stations, plazas, empty lots, existing drive-thrus.
  • Competition: the 23 Tim Hortons in the official directory. A McDonald's with a drive-thru on the corner proves zoning allows it.
  • For each corner, one exact target (lot for sale, pad in a plaza parking lot or empty unit) with the owner or broker.

Rules to know

  • The zoning bylaw generally allows drive-thrus, but has banned them in some zones since 2025 (section 652): check each lot's zoning grid before making an offer.
  • Since 2025 the City no longer requires a minimum number of parking spaces for many businesses: easier to fit a small building into a plaza.
  • Chemin Vanier, between Pink and chemin d'Aylmer: interim control bylaw (setbacks) and a widening study running to 2028.
  • West-end tramway (Plateau and Aylmer): construction not before about 2030, with the Plateau first. Worth watching for the Allumettières corners.

Next steps

  • One call to First Capital (Alexandre Lauzon) covers 2 corners: Carrefour du Plateau and Place Nelligan.
  • Ask the 6 Lorrain broker whether it can be bought with 867 Maloney Est, and the 534 Saint-Joseph broker for a site plan with a drive-thru lane.
  • Find the owners of the Metro plaza (Wilfrid-Lavigne) and the northwest Lorrain × La Vérendrye lot in the assessment roll.
  • Visit the top 5 on a weekday morning, 7 to 9 a.m., driving in the commuters' direction.
